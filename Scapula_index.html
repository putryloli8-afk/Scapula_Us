A little about us

Because every little moment deserves to be remembered.

Scapula & Us was created from a simple thought: memories should be more than scattered photographs on a screen. They deserve a place where they can be collected, revisited, and treasured.

We believe that every story is different. A first anniversary, a childhood journey, a friendship, a family gathering, or the little everyday moments — each deserves its own beautiful space.

Our purpose is to turn your personal stories into meaningful keepsakes, whether in physical or digital form.

Frequently asked questions

Things you might wonder.

Can I fully customize my memory book?

Yes. You can discuss your preferred theme, colors, cover design, page layout, size, and personal content with our team.

Do I need to prepare my own photographs?

Yes, you can provide your selected photographs and materials. We can help arrange them into a beautiful layout.

Can I order a physical and digital version?

Yes. You can request a physical memory book, a digital memory book, or a combination of both.

How much does a memory book cost?

Pricing depends on the format, size, number of pages, materials, binding, and level of customization. Contact us for a personalized quotation.

How long does production take?

Production time depends on design complexity, revision rounds, and printing requirements. An estimated timeline will be provided when your order is confirmed.

Can I make a memory book as a gift?

Absolutely. You can create a personalized keepsake for anniversaries, birthdays, graduations, friendships, family celebrations, and other meaningful occasions.
